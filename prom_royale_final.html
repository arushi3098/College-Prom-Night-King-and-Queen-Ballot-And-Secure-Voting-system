Good to know

Questions, answered.

Details about how this ballot works.

Can I change my choices before confirming?＋

Yes. Choose another nominee in either category before you submit the ballot.

Will my choices stay saved?＋

Yes, this version uses localStorage in the current browser. It is not sent to a server and can be cleared or modified.

Is this secure enough for a real election?＋

No. A real election requires verified student login, server-side duplicate-vote prevention, a protected database, privacy controls, and secured admin access.
